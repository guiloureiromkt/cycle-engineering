## What the schema actually is (learned from the tool, not guessed)
- `claude plugin eval init <name>` writes the **`prompt.md` + `graders/*.md`** form. Its frontmatter accepts only: `schema_version, name, description, tags, plugins, runs, expected_outcome, model, max_turns, timeout_seconds, allowed_tools, artifact_publish, growthbook_overrides, append_system_prompt, env` — **no `scaffold_script`**. Every case of ours needs a fixture, so that form cannot be used.
- The **`case.yaml`** form takes it: `schema_version`, `name`, `description`, `runs`, `execution.{prompt, scaffold_script, allowed_tools, max_turns}`, and `graders: [...]`.
- A grader needs **`name`** and `type`. Types: `regex · tool_order · tool_used · file_exists · llm · baseline`. `with-only` and `input_contains` are **not** grader keys (rejected by the loader); `tool_used` takes `tool:` and counts calls.
- `--trust-plugin` is required on every non-interactive run in this repo; `--scaffold` is off by default and the run does nothing useful without it.
